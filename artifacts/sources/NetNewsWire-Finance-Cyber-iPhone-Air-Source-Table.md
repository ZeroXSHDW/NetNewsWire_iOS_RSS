## Profile notes

The iPhone Air profile contains 125 feeds: the Lite alert and triage core plus compact Canadian CSE, Global Affairs, Defence Investment Agency and CSIS streams, CIS MS-ISAC Advisories, the European Council meeting calendar, RUSI Latest Commentary, SIPRI Global Security & Arms Control, U.S. Courts Judiciary News, the English Banco Central do Brasil Focus Market Readout, Apple Newsroom and targeted Irish, EU, UK, US and cyber-research streams. It is the recommended daily profile; the device budget caps it at 125 feeds, 4 MB of full feed bodies, 600 KB per feed, six mobile-review feeds and four interrupting notifications, while the digest handoff caps the selected input at 30 items and 90,000 text characters. Larger Canadian National Defence, the other Council meeting calendars, Chatham House research and the new UK Government national-security and cyber-security taxonomy streams remain in Master for the local digest collector.
